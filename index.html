How it’s encrypted

Only your master password opens the vault.

Encryption happens on your iPhone or Mac. Nothing readable is ever stored or uploaded.

  1. 1
    Master password

    Known only to you. Never stored or sent.

  2. 2
    Argon2id

    64 MiB, 3 passes — makes guessing slow and expensive.

  3. 3
    Vault key

    A random 256-bit key, wrapped by your master key.

  4. 4
    AES-256-GCM

    Seals your vault and each file with its own key.

  5. 5
    Encrypted vault

    Stored on your device and, if you choose, your Google Drive.

Tamper-proof. Authenticated encryption detects any change to your data.
Fast password changes. Only the vault key is re-wrapped; Face ID keeps working.
Passcode lockout. After 5 wrong attempts, only the master password works.
Don’t trust us — verify. The open-source Vault Viewer decrypts a backup entirely in your browser, offline, with a separate implementation of the same format.

Sync on your terms

Every device. Your Google Drive.

Sync is optional. When it’s on, an encrypted copy lives in a private app folder in your own Drive — hidden from the Drive app, readable only with your master password.

1

Turn on sync

Settings → Google Drive → sign in. Your encrypted vault uploads.

2

Add another device

Install CabinetPass → Import Existing Vault → From Google Drive → enter your master password.

3

Stay in sync

Changes merge item by item across devices. Status and any issues show right on the Vaults screen.

Prefer offline? Export an encrypted backup file anytime and restore it on a new iPhone.

Data Not Collected.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no account. We don’t run servers that hold your data, so there’s nothing for us to see, sell or lose.

  • Your vault stays on your device unless you turn on sync.
  • Sync stores only encrypted data, in your own Google Drive.
  • Breach checks send a 5-character hash prefix — never your password.

Questions

What if I forget my master password?

It can’t be recovered — not by you, and not by us. That’s what keeps your vault private. Choose a passphrase of five or more random words and keep a written copy somewhere safe.

Can Google read my passwords?

No. The vault is encrypted on your iPhone before upload. Google stores an encrypted file in a hidden app folder and never has the key.

Can I change my master password?

Yes — Settings → Change Master Password. Your other synced devices pick up the new password automatically.

How do I move from another password manager?

Export a CSV from Chrome, Safari, Bitwarden, 1Password or LastPass, then import it in Settings. Delete the CSV afterwards — it isn’t encrypted.

What happens after 2 GB of files?

Everything you’ve saved stays available — you can still view, edit, copy, sync and fill it, and you can keep adding passwords and notes. To attach more files, upgrade to Pro for $0.99 a month or $39.99 once.

What files can I store?

PDFs, photos, camera scans and common documents up to 50 MB each. Every file is encrypted with its own key.

Can I check the encryption myself?

Yes. The file format is documented and the Vault Viewer is open source on GitHub. It decrypts an exported backup offline in your browser, using code written separately from the app.
